## 核心公式速览

### 一、矢量分析基础

| 运算 | 公式 |
|------|------|
| 梯度 | \\(\nabla \phi = \frac{\partial \phi}{\partial x}\hat{x} + \frac{\partial \phi}{\partial y}\hat{y} + \frac{\partial \phi}{\partial z}\hat{z}\\) |
| 散度 | \\(\nabla \cdot \mathbf{A} = \frac{\partial A_x}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial A_y}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial A_z}{\partial z}\\) |
| 旋度 | \\(\nabla \times \mathbf{A} = \begin{vmatrix} \hat{x} & \hat{y} & \hat{z} \\ \partial_x & \partial_y & \partial_z \\ A_x & A_y & A_z \end{vmatrix}\\) |
| 高斯散度定理 | \\(\oint_S \mathbf{A} \cdot d\mathbf{S} = \int_V (\nabla \cdot \mathbf{A}) dV\\) |
| 斯托克斯定理 | \\(\oint_C \mathbf{A} \cdot d\mathbf{l} = \int_S (\nabla \times \mathbf{A}) \cdot d\mathbf{S}\\) |

### 二、静电场

**基本方程：**
\\[
\nabla \cdot \mathbf{D} = \rho, \quad \nabla \times \mathbf{E} = 0
\\]

**电位与电场关系：** \\(\mathbf{E} = -\nabla \varphi\\)

**泊松方程与拉普拉斯方程：**
\\[
\nabla^2 \varphi = -\frac{\rho}{\varepsilon} \quad \text{(有源)}, \qquad
\nabla^2 \varphi = 0 \quad \text{(无源)}
\\]

**介质分界面衔接条件：**
- \\(E_{1t} = E_{2t}\\)（电场切向连续）
- \\(D_{1n} - D_{2n} = \rho_s\\)（电位移法向突变）

### 三、恒定磁场

**基本方程：**
\\[
\nabla \cdot \mathbf{B} = 0, \quad \nabla \times \mathbf{H} = \mathbf{J}
\\]

**安培环路定律：** \\(\oint_C \mathbf{H} \cdot d\mathbf{l} = I\\)

**磁介质本构关系：** \\(\mathbf{B} = \mu \mathbf{H} = \mu_0 (\mathbf{H} + \mathbf{M})\\)

### 四、时变电磁场（麦克斯韦方程组）

**积分形式：**
\\[
\oint_C \mathbf{H} \cdot d\mathbf{l} = \int_S (\mathbf{J} + \frac{\partial \mathbf{D}}{\partial t}) \cdot d\mathbf{S}
\\]
\\[
\oint_C \mathbf{E} \cdot d\mathbf{l} = -\int_S \frac{\partial \mathbf{B}}{\partial t} \cdot d\mathbf{S}
\\]
\\[
\oint_S \mathbf{D} \cdot d\mathbf{S} = \int_V \rho dV
\\]
\\[
\oint_S \mathbf{B} \cdot d\mathbf{S} = 0
\\]

**微分形式：**
\\[
\nabla \times \mathbf{H} = \mathbf{J} + \frac{\partial \mathbf{D}}{\partial t}, \quad
\nabla \times \mathbf{E} = -\frac{\partial \mathbf{B}}{\partial t}
\\]
\\[
\nabla \cdot \mathbf{D} = \rho, \quad \nabla \cdot \mathbf{B} = 0
\\]

**坡印廷定理：**
\\[
\nabla \cdot (\mathbf{E} \times \mathbf{H}) = -\frac{\partial}{\partial t} \left( \frac{1}{2}\varepsilon E^2 + \frac{1}{2}\mu H^2 \right) - \mathbf{J} \cdot \mathbf{E}
\\]

### 五、时谐场

**复矢量表示：** \\(\mathbf{E}(x,y,z,t) = \text{Re}[\dot{\mathbf{E}}(x,y,z) e^{j\omega t}]\\)

**波动方程：**
\\[
\nabla^2 \dot{\mathbf{E}} + k^2 \dot{\mathbf{E}} = 0, \quad k = \omega \sqrt{\mu\varepsilon}
\\]

**趋肤深度：** \\(\delta = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi f \mu \sigma}}\\)
